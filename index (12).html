Our first book · February 2027

Lizzie and Her White-Tipped Tail

Lizzie is a young fox living in a dark den beneath Hyde Park, while everyone she knows seems to live somewhere brighter. She wants a home she can invite her friends into, and she is determined to find her own way to make one.

A pourquoi tale about earning your place, set on London streets a child can recognise, and painted entirely in watercolour.

Advisory services

For debut publishers and newcomers to the industry

Setting up an imprint means learning a hundred things nobody writes down: what a printer needs in a quote, how ISBNs and trade metadata actually work, what an illustrator contract must contain, and why a book priced wrongly can never make money. We have worked through all of it recently, and we advise others doing the same.

01

Manuscript and structure

Pagination across a 32 page extent, pacing the page turns, and weighing contradictory editorial notes without losing the book.

02

Editorial and copy

Line editing for read-aloud, and how to brief a professional editor so the money buys what you actually need.

03

Illustration

Finding and commissioning an illustrator, art direction, rights buyouts, and contracts that hold up later.

04

Print production

Offset specification, sourcing and vetting printers in Asia, proofing, freight, duty and true landed cost per copy.

05

Imprint set-up and metadata

Registering an imprint, ISBN blocks, Nielsen and trade databases, barcodes, and the records retailers actually read.

06

Rights and route to market

Distribution, translation rights, book fairs, direct sales, and pricing that still leaves a margin at the end.

Submissions

We are accepting picture book manuscripts

We publish picture books for ages two to seven and nothing else, so please send us only that. We take on very few, but every submission is read by a person and every writer receives an answer.

We never ask an author to contribute to the cost of publication. Illustration, printing and distribution are paid for by us. Our advisory service is entirely separate and is never a condition of being published.
Submit your manuscript
  1. Send your workThe complete text with a short note about who you are and who the book is for. One file, Word or PDF.
  2. We acknowledge itWithin five working days, so you know it arrived and is not sitting in a filter.
  3. We read it aloudA picture book has to survive being read to a child four hundred times. That is the first test.
  4. We answer properlyWithin eight weeks, with written notes on what is working and what is not. Not a form rejection.
  5. We make an offerAdvance and royalty terms, a proposed illustrator, and a publication slot.

About us

Made slowly, on purpose

DreamDoor Books was established in London in 2026 to publish picture books for the years when a child is read to rather than reading alone. Our stories are set where children already are, in parks and on pavements and at bus stops, so the animals in them live in the same city as the reader.

Every illustration is painted by hand in watercolour. Our books are printed by offset litho and Smyth sewn, so they open flat and survive the shelf. Our first title has been named a finalist in two international children's book awards ahead of publication.
